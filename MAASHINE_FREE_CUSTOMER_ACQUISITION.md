# MaaShine Cleaning Services — Zero-Budget Customer Acquisition Playbook
**Local Market:** Mysore, Karnataka | **Business Model:** Single Professional Cleaning Team

---

## Executive Summary
This document outlines a practical, 100% free, zero-paid-ads customer acquisition playbook for MaaShine in Mysore. Every channel described below requires zero software subscriptions, zero ad spend, and zero third-party marketing tools.

---

## Core Acquisition Channels

### 1. Google Business Profile (Local SEO #1 Priority)
- **Why it matters:** When someone in Mysore searches *"deep cleaning near me"*, *"house cleaning Gokulam"*, or *"sofa cleaning Mysore"*, Google Maps and local 3-pack search results appear at the very top.
- **Action Items:**
  1. Claim your free profile at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com).
  2. Set Primary Category: **Cleaning Service** / **House Cleaning Service**.
  3. Set Service Areas: *Gokulam, Vijayanagar, Kuvempunagar, Jayalakshmipuram, VV Mohalla, Saraswathipuram, Hebbal, JP Nagar, Dattagalli, Bogadi, Yadavagiri*.
  4. Add website link: `https://maashineservices.com` and Booking URL: `https://maashineservices.com/booking`.
  5. Upload 10–15 high-quality, authentic photos of cleaning equipment, uniforms/staff, and real before/after results (with customer permission).
  6. **Google Reviews:** After every completed job, ask the customer to leave an honest 5-star review on your Google profile. 10 authentic reviews will dramatically boost local ranking.

---

### 2. WhatsApp Status & Direct Contact Strategy
- **Why it matters:** In Mysore, WhatsApp is the primary daily communication platform for homeowners, apartment residents, and local business owners.
- **Action Items:**
  1. **Weekly Status Rotations (2–3 times/week):** Post short before/after photo slides or video snippets of sparkling clean kitchens, descaled bathrooms, or deep-cleaned sofas.
  2. **Direct Status CTA:** *"Getting your home cleaned this weekend? Message us on WhatsApp or book at maashineservices.com"*.
  3. **Customer Follow-Up (Day +3):** Send a courteous check-in message asking if everything remains spotless and invite them to share MaaShine with neighbors via `maashineservices.com/refer`.

---

### 3. Mysore Apartment Communities & Residents Associations (RWA)
- **Why it matters:** Apartment complexes in Vijayanagar, Gokulam, Hebbal, and Dattagalli have high demand for deep cleaning during move-ins, move-outs, and festival seasons.
- **Action Items:**
  1. **Introduce to Association Secretary/Admin:** Reach out respectfully with a digital flyer introducing MaaShine as a reliable, vetted local cleaning team.
  2. **Apartment Noticeboard / Community Groups (MyGate / ApnaComplex / WhatsApp):** Share a helpful message about move-in deep cleaning and water-stain bathroom descaling.
  3. **Cluster Booking Advantage:** If 2–3 apartments in the same building book on consecutive slots, offer scheduled convenience.

---

### 4. Local Office & Commercial Outreach
- **Why it matters:** Clinics, law offices, architecture firms, tutoring centers, and IT/startup offices in Saraswathipuram, Jayalakshmipuram, and Hebbal require recurring bi-weekly or monthly deep cleaning.
- **Action Items:**
  1. Direct introduction letter or WhatsApp message to office managers.
  2. Emphasize weekend/after-hours scheduling so their regular workday is not disrupted.
  3. Provide official GST/invoicing through `maashineservices.com/invoice/[id]`.

---

### 5. Word-of-Mouth & Customer Referral Engine
- **Why it matters:** Word-of-mouth is the highest-converting marketing channel in residential services.
- **Action Items:**
  1. Send your dedicated referral link: `https://maashineservices.com/refer`.
  2. Encourage customers to forward the link into their family and apartment WhatsApp groups.
  3. The `/refer` page provides instant 1-click WhatsApp and Web Share buttons.

---

### 6. Local Community & Social Media Sharing (Instagram & Facebook Groups)
- **Why it matters:** Mysore local groups (e.g. *Mysoreans, Living in Mysore, Mysore Buy/Sell/Services*) frequently have members asking: *"Can anyone recommend a good deep cleaning service in Mysore?"*
- **Action Items:**
  1. Set up a clean Instagram profile `@maashineservices` with bio link `maashineservices.com`.
  2. Post genuine short reels of cleaning transformations (e.g., grease-free kitchen chimney, stain-free sofa extraction).
  3. Monitor local Facebook groups for people asking for cleaning recommendations and reply helpfully with your website and phone number.

## What NOT To Do (Zero-Tolerance Rules)
- ❌ **Do NOT buy fake reviews or ratings:** Google and customers can detect fabricated reviews. Build authentic credibility with genuine satisfied clients.
- ❌ **Do NOT spam WhatsApp groups:** Never post unsolicited advertisements in groups where promotional posts are banned. Always seek admin permission or post in dedicated service threads.
- ❌ **Do NOT over-promise on scheduling:** Because MaaShine operates with a single dedicated team, always ensure slots are confirmed accurately through `maashineservices.com/booking` to prevent double-booking.
- ❌ **Do NOT use paid marketing platforms prematurely:** Before spending any money on paid ads, exhaust all free Google, WhatsApp, and community referral channels.
